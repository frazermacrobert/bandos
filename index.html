About Christmas Nandos Traitors

Each round, you'll face a Christmas Nandos scenario about being a good mate.
1️⃣ Pick the good lad behaviour — avoid being a dickhead.
2️⃣ Shit lad behaviour means instant elimination.
3️⃣ Choose well to see what everyone else did that round.
4️⃣ Vote off the player you suspect of being a traitor to the group.
5️⃣ Keep going until all traitors are gone (win) or they take over (lose).
If votes tie, the group decides automatically.
